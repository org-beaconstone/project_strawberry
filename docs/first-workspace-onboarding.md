# Strawberry first-workspace onboarding

## Initial workspace

Each new workspace starts with one editable example board demonstrating
a small collaboration workflow.

Users can rename, edit, or delete the board.

## Teammate invitations

During setup, users can invite a teammate or skip the invitation step.
Users who skip can invite teammates later from the workspace.

Access to the board does not depend on sending an invitation.

## Scope

This specification covers first-workspace onboarding for Strawberry’s
freemium launch. Billing behaviour and free-tier limits are unchanged.
